## Notes

- Maker asks are seeded before the measured window; offered taker commands and fill rates exclude maker setup.
- Each taker buy crosses pre-rested one-unit maker asks, so one accepted taker command produces one fill.
- Fills are counted from CommandResponse.fills and cross-checked against WebSocket Trade frames.
- The pacer stops at the duration boundary, so acknowledged fills can be a few ticks above or below the nominal target.
- The end-to-end probe uses a separate market and fixed-market WebSocket, so probe deltas are unambiguous.
- Gateway command counters include the low-rate probe and are cumulative since gateway start; scripts/load-test.sh starts a fresh gateway.
- Loopback HTTP/WS with no TLS, proxy, or injected network delay.
